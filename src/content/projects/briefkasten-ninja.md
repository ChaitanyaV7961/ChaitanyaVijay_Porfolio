## Overview

I worked on Briefkasten Ninja, an AI document-processing and triage workflow built around n8n, Gemini and LangChain. The idea is to turn incoming administrative documents into structured information and actionable next steps.

</section>

<section id="problem">

## Problem

Administrative documents can arrive in different formats and require manual review, classification, information extraction and follow-up. The project explores how an AI-assisted workflow can reduce repetitive handling while retaining human oversight.

</section>

<section id="approach">

## Approach

The workflow combines document intake, language-model-based understanding, structured extraction and routing. Human review remains important for uncertain or sensitive cases.

</section>
